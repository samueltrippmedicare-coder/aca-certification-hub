Federal Marketplace Resources

If you sell in a state that uses the Federally-facilitated Marketplace (FFM) on HealthCare.gov, you complete CMS Marketplace registration and training once through CMS. The same federal process also covers State-Based Marketplaces on the Federal Platform (SBM-FP) — but an SBM-FP state may still add its own requirements on top (Arkansas does; see its card below).

Important for PY2027 Federal registration moratorium for new agents and brokers — blocked by court

CMS placed a temporary moratorium on new agent and broker registration with the Federally-facilitated Exchanges for Plan Year 2027, effective September 22, 2026. On November 5, 2026, a federal court blocked it.

  • Court ruling: the U.S. District Court for the District of Maryland granted a preliminary injunction to the National Association of Benefits and Insurance Professionals (NABIP) and Health Agents for America, finding they are likely to succeed on their Administrative Procedure Act claims. NABIP sued HHS and CMS on October 15, 2026. According to NABIP, the ruling lifts the moratorium immediately and PY2027 training for new agents and brokers began November 6, 2026.
  • What new agents should do: if you did not hold a PY2026 Exchange agreement, log in to the MLMS through the CMS Enterprise Portal and check whether PY2027 registration and training is available to you. A preliminary injunction is not a final decision — the case continues and CMS may appeal — so confirm your status with CMS before you enroll clients.
  • Who the moratorium covered: agents and brokers who did not hold Plan Year 2026 Exchange agreements with the Federally-facilitated Exchanges (about 19,000 people, by CMS’s estimate). CMS would not execute PY2027 Exchange agreements with them, including the SHOP agreement, while it was in place.
  • Who it never covered: returning agents and brokers who held PY2026 Exchange agreements — PY2027 registration and training has been open to them throughout. It also did not apply to web-brokers, or to agents reinstated after a terminated or denied agreement.
  • Original end date: CMS had scheduled the moratorium to run until February 1, 2027. If the injunction is overturned, that schedule could apply again.
  • State-Based Marketplaces on their own platforms are not subject to it. CMS states the moratorium does not affect registration on the State-Based Exchanges. A new agent may also certify directly with a state that runs its own Marketplace — subject to that state's own rules and windows.
  • Arkansas and Oklahoma use the federal platform (SBM-FP), so agents there register through CMS and were covered by the moratorium.

PY2027 federal deadline: CMS has not published a single cutoff date, but you cannot enroll clients through HealthCare.gov until your PY2027 registration and training is complete. Open Enrollment runs November 1, 2026 – January 15, 2027, so finish as soon as possible. Returning agents who do not complete PY2027 registration and training will lose Marketplace access. The training system normally closes in mid-summer (PY2026 closed July 12, 2026); CMS has not yet announced the PY2027 closing date.

Always verify each state's separate Marketplace requirements before assuming you are ready to sell there.

State-Based Resources

These jurisdictions run their own State-Based Marketplace or require a separate state certification in addition to (or instead of) the federal process. Marketplace certification is not the same as being carrier-appointed — in most states you must contract with each carrier separately before you are ready to sell. Find your state below.

🔍

Arkansas

Federal platform + state license

Arkansas Health Insurance Marketplace

Arkansas enrolls through HealthCare.gov, but CMS certification alone does not make you ready to sell here. You also need an Arkansas Exchange Producer license from the Arkansas Insurance Department, on top of an active Arkansas Life & Health license. Each year you must complete CMS/FFM training and renew the Exchange Producer license — renewals are due by September 30. The state “Pathways to Enrollment” course is required for initial licensure only; for annual renewal it is recommended but not required.

California

Covered California

You need a California Accident & Health or Sickness license and separate Covered California Certified Insurance Agent certification — federal certification does not apply. Independent agents are treated as an “agency of one” and sign the Agency Agreement as agency manager; agents working under an agency sign the Non-Monetary Agreement. Complete the Covered California University curriculum and pass the open-book exam with 80% or higher (three attempts), upload your E&O certificate, pay the CDI endorsement fee, and create your Enroller Portal credentials to finish. Appoint with each carrier separately or commissions will not pay.

Colorado

Connect for Health Colorado

Scheduling trap: certification training is unavailable each year from August 1–31 and again from October 16 – December 31.

You need a current Colorado Accident & Health license (resident or non-resident), E&O of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence / $1,000,000 aggregate, an electronically signed Producer’s Agreement, and completion of the online certification coursework in the C4U portal. Recertification is required at least annually; brokers who completed the prior training by July 31 get a shorter returning curriculum, otherwise you take the full one. Colorado-resident brokers can earn CE credit for the coursework. Contact each issuer directly for appointment before submitting enrollments.

Connecticut

Access Health CT

Connecticut reverses the usual order: you must already hold a current Connecticut producer license and already be appointed with the participating carriers before Access Health CT will send your agreement. Once credentials are in place, email your name as it appears on your license, NPN, email and phone to BrokerRegistration.AHCT@ct.gov; AHCT verifies, then its learning system releases the agreement. Training modules and the assessment unlock only after you sign, and for PY2027 you must score 80 or higher with two attempts. Certification is annual, completed in the fall before the plan year.

District of Columbia

DC Health Link

A DC producer license is only the first of four requirements. You must also register with DCHBX for Approved Broker status, obtain appointments with every licensed carrier entity in each market you intend to sell (Individual & Family, Small Business, or both), and complete two separate Marketplace trainings. Those trainings renew on different clocks: privacy and security training is required annually, while the policy and systems training — administered jointly with NABIP — is required every two years.

Georgia

Georgia Access

Georgia Access is now an established State-Based Marketplace in its second plan year on its own platform. You need an active Georgia Accident & Sickness license, completion of Georgia Access certification training, and a passing score of 80% or higher on the final assessment. Create your Agent Portal profile and establish appointments with each participating QHP and SADP issuer, then enroll clients through the Agent Portal or an approved EDE partner. Certification is plan-year specific — PY2027 training does not satisfy PY2026 — and PY2027 certification is available until July 31, 2027. Resident agents earn CE credit (three credits if new, one if returning).

Idaho

Your Health Idaho

You must become a Your Health Idaho “Certified Partner” on top of holding an Idaho license in good standing with the Department of Insurance. Complete the training modules in YHI’s Learning Management System and pass the final examination with a minimum score of 90% within three attempts — the strictest exam standard of any state Marketplace. You also review and attest to the Your Health Idaho Producer Agreement. Agents and brokers are eligible to receive continuing education units for completing the certification courses.

Illinois

Get Covered Illinois

Illinois has operated its own State-Based Marketplace platform since Plan Year 2026, so PY2027 is business as usual. You need a Health line of authority with the Illinois Department of Insurance, a Get Covered Illinois Broker Portal account, and completion of the annual certification training including a final exam passed at 80% or higher. The final step is attesting to the Illinois Non-Exchange Entity Agreement. PY2027 certification is valid through December 31, 2027. Returning brokers have been pre-registered; new brokers request pre-registration by emailing GCI.Outreach@Illinois.gov. Illinois-resident brokers earn five CE credits.

Kentucky

Refresher window closed

kynect (Kentucky Health Benefit Exchange)

The shortened PY2027 Refresher Training has closed — but you are not locked out of Kentucky. KHBE states that agents who did not complete the refresher and still want to sell on the Kentucky State-Based Exchange for PY2027 must instead take the full Agent Certification Training. You need a Kentucky license with a health line of authority, registration through the Kentucky Online Gateway, and completion of training within three attempts. You must also hold (or be designated under an entity holding) an appointment with at least one participating QHP or SADP issuer.

Maine

CoverME.gov

Hold an active Maine license with health authority, complete the CoverME.gov online broker training in its learning system and pass the final exam, then register for certification and accept the Marketplace privacy and security requirements. Budget time for state review — CoverME.gov states the gap between registration and certification can take 7–10 days while staff verify your training and licensure. One practical note: the consumer-facing “Find Local Help” directory listing is only for certified brokers physically located in Maine, so non-resident brokers get certification but not the listing.

Maryland

Apply before Nov 1

Maryland Health Connection

No new broker authorization applications can be submitted during Open Enrollment, November 1, 2026 – January 15, 2027. If Maryland matters to you for PY2027, apply before November 1, 2026.

You need a current Maryland license qualifying you to sell health benefits, E&O coverage of $1M per claim and up to $1M per year, completion of MHBE’s Broker Authorization training, and a passing score on the Consumer Assistance Exam. Federal or other-state exchange training cannot be substituted. Once authorized you must train annually and file a renewal application every other year, with at least eight hours of MHBE-approved CE per renewal term. Maryland also expects you to be contracted with all participating carriers and to present every carrier’s plans.

Massachusetts

Small group only

Massachusetts Health Connector

Massachusetts is the outlier. Health Connector broker certification covers the small-group market only — Health Connector for Business, employers with 1–50 employees. Its Broker Agreement directs certified brokers to refer individual and non-group consumers, including anyone asking about subsidies, to Navigators, Certified Application Counselors, or the Health Connector directly. There is no individual-and-family broker certification program to enroll in. For the small-group side you need a Massachusetts DOI producer license for health and dental, a certification request, a W-9 and account setup, and the Health Connector’s self-paced training, with 7–10 business days for review.

Minnesota

MNsure

Selling on MNsure requires a separate annual certification on top of an active Minnesota producer license. Your agency registers first through the Broker Agency Management Program, then you are added to its roster, agree to the MNsure Code of Conduct, and complete the required online courses in MNsure’s learning system plus a passing assessment. The courses award three CE credits, reported to Sircon within 10 days. Recertification is due by MNsure’s October deadline to stay certified for Open Enrollment — but unlike several other states, MNsure lets brokers register and certify at any point during the year.

Nevada

PY2027 intake closed

Nevada Health Link

The Intent to Sell (ITS) period for Plan Year 2027 is now closed. New brokers cannot opt in for PY2027; the next ITS form is posted in June.

Nevada gates new-broker access through an Intent to Sell window rather than open enrollment in the training. For PY2027, Nevada Health Link invites Nevada-resident brokers and certified non-resident brokers who have an active Nevada Health Link book of business. Certified non-resident brokers without an active NVHL book are not invited. Returning brokers certified for PY2026 skip the ITS form entirely and receive their training link automatically in September, with the certification course due by October 15.

New Jersey

Get Covered New Jersey

You need a New Jersey producer license plus separate annual Marketplace certification. New brokers submit the Broker Intent to Sell form, allow up to seven days for training access, complete the required training modules, and sign the GetCoveredNJ Broker Acknowledgment; returning brokers retrain before each Open Enrollment. Complete the form and training a week prior to November 1 to be certified when Open Enrollment opens — finish later and you are certified on a rolling basis rather than shut out. Certification is not the same as ready-to-sell: you are separately responsible for being appointed with every carrier offering plans on the exchange.

New Mexico

PY2027 window closed

BeWell (beWellnm)

PY2027 BeWell certification ran July 1 – August 31, 2026 and that window has closed.

BeWell has an unusual geographic gate: you must hold an active New Mexico resident license, or be domiciled in an approved bordering state — Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico, Oklahoma, Texas or Utah — with an active license in that state. Certification is annual and does not carry forward from the prior year, and you must pass the BeWell certification training with 80% or greater. Carrier contracting is entirely on you: BeWell states it does not assist with or participate in broker contracts, and you must be appointed or contracted to receive commissions.

New York

NY State of Health

You need a current NY Department of Financial Services license in good standing plus NY State of Health’s own free online certification. Create a NY.gov ID through the Register Broker Account link, let NYSOH verify your license with DFS, then complete the certification courses NYSOH emails you. Certification covers two markets: you must pass the exam at the end of both the Small Business Marketplace and Individual Marketplace courses, and Small Business certification is a prerequisite for the Individual courses. Unlike most exchanges, NYSOH recertification is required every two years rather than annually, and carries no CE credit.

Oregon

Oct 31 training deadline

Explore Health – Oregon Health Insurance Marketplace

Currently licensed agents must complete Oregon’s Marketplace certification training by October 31, 2026. Agents onboarded after that date should complete it as soon as possible, preferably within 30 days.

Starting November 1, 2026, Oregon leaves HealthCare.gov and enrolls PY2027 business on its own platform, Explore Health — do not assume the Oregon workflow still runs on HealthCare.gov. You need an active Oregon producer license with a health line of authority plus Oregon’s annual Marketplace Certification Training, which requires 80% or higher on the online exam and is what grants and maintains access to the new state system. There is no abbreviated refresher this cycle; because the curriculum is new for the transition, the full course must be completed even if you certified before. Keep your CMS registration current too — you still need it to access HealthCare.gov for pre-2027 Oregon enrollments and to sell in states that remain on the federal platform.

Pennsylvania

Nov 30 recert deadline

Pennie

Brokers currently certified through 12/31/2026 must complete PY2027 recertification by November 30, 2026. Miss it and Pennie decertifies you effective 12/31/2026 and your customers are de-designated. You can still certify afterward, but your account is treated as new.

Pennie requires its own annual broker certification on top of your Pennsylvania producer license with the Accident & Health or Sickness qualification. Certification is free and delivered online through TrainPA: create or update your account, complete the broker modules in order, pass the final exam, and attest to the Broker Agreement. Pennie then verifies your license — approvals are processed at least weekly — before your status moves from Pending to Certified. Brand-new Pennie brokers are not subject to the November 30 deadline and can certify at any time.

Rhode Island

Group market only

HealthSource RI

Rhode Island works differently from other state exchanges. HealthSource RI states that its broker certification is for group and employer-sponsored insurance only, delivered through HealthSource RI for Employers, and that carriers do not compensate or pay commissions to brokers for individual and family products. Rhode Island is therefore not a conventional commission-paying individual-market opportunity; broker revenue there comes from small-group and non-profit employer business. You need an active Rhode Island producer license with accident & health authority and completion of a scheduled live certification class; off-cycle credentialing is available by emailing Brokers@HealthSourceRI.com.

Vermont

Assister program

Vermont Health Connect

Vermont does not run a conventional annual Marketplace certification, but it is not correct to say it has no credentialing process either. Vermont Health Connect operates a Certified Assister program, and licensed insurance brokers are one of three recognized assister categories — the only one permitted to charge the consumer a fee. Holding a Vermont producer license does not automatically make you a Certified Assister; VHC states plainly that not all brokers are. Producers holding a Vermont resident license may apply: the process is an online application, DVHA privacy and security training, a training course and open-book exam (about six hours total), and an agreement with VHC, followed by annual recertification.

Virginia

Virginia’s Insurance Marketplace

Virginia requires Virginia-specific certification and will not accept your HealthCare.gov certification. You need an active Virginia license with a health qualification in good standing, an agent portal account on the Marketplace platform (independent agents create a one-person “agency” using their SSN as the EIN), and completion of agent training in the Marketplace LMS, with a passing score of 80 on the final exam as currently published. Your portal account stays “pending” until the Exchange verifies your license and training. Agents who completed PY2026 certification are auto-enrolled in a shorter recertification course; new agents take the full program. Virginia has not published a PY2027 deadline — confirm your status in the LMS.

Washington

$50 fee · training closed

Washington Healthplanfinder

For the current cycle the payment deadline was October 1, 2026 and training closed October 16, 2026, with a blackout through January 2027. New producer training resumes February 2027.

You need an active Washington producer license with disability lines of authority, and you must be appointed by each carrier whose plans you sell — the Exchange itself pays no commissions. Certification means paying a $50 fee, which applies to new and recertifying producers alike and is non-refundable, completing the required training in the Exchange’s learning system (each producer needs an individual account; agency accounts are not available), and returning a signed user-access agreement by DocuSign. Expect about two to three business days for payment processing and again for your DocuSign after training. Recertification is required annually or your account is terminated.
