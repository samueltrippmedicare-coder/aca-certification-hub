01 / Before you launch

Make the key decisions.

An Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement (ICHRA), also called a CHOICE Arrangement by CMS, is an employer-funded health benefit that reimburses eligible medical expenses.

This guide follows the simplest operating model: an employer reimburses qualifying individual insurance premiums, and employees purchase their own off-exchange ACA plans. Employers of different sizes can offer an ICHRA; there is no universal minimum employer size or federal ICHRA contribution cap.

Employer

Design and administer the benefit

Choose the allowance and eligible classes, adopt the documents, designate an administrator and backup, approve claims, coordinate payroll, protect records, and handle reporting.

Employee + licensed agent

Choose individual coverage

Review doctors, medications, networks, premiums, and enrollment eligibility. The employee chooses and maintains the policy and submits the required proof.

Write down these decisions first

  • Business and people: legal employer name, EIN, locations, employee census, related businesses, owner tax status, and who will administer claims.
  • Timing: plan year, effective date, new-hire eligibility and any waiting period. Schedule notices before promising a start date; applicable waiting-period limits still apply.
  • Benefit: monthly/annual allowance, eligible family members, premium-only expenses, when funds become available, carryover or forfeiture, claim submission deadlines, and termination rules.
  • Operations: a secure submission method, review and appeal contacts, a reimbursement ledger, payroll codes, and a tax/reporting owner.
  • Optional payroll: decide whether to sponsor a separate Section 125 plan for employees’ unreimbursed off-exchange premiums.

No ongoing administrator required does not mean no setup help. You can use a benefits attorney or qualified document provider for a one-time plan package and have your own staff run it. Your payroll provider and tax preparer can handle their respective tasks without becoming the ICHRA administrator.

This is a federal educational roadmap, not an adopted plan document or a determination that a particular employer is compliant. State requirements and the employer’s workforce, ownership, and benefit design can add duties.

02 / Eligibility & allowances

Define eligible employees fairly.

“Permitted classes” are the categories an employer may use to divide its workforce for ICHRA offers. These rules apply to every ICHRA sponsor. Do not select employees based on health, expected claims, or who would be most expensive to insure.

Which employee classes are permitted?

The regulation permits full-time; part-time; salaried; non-salaried; seasonal; employees covered by a collective bargaining agreement; employees who have not satisfied a waiting period; certain nonresident aliens without U.S.-source earned income; employees whose primary worksite is in the same insurance rating area; and qualifying temporary staffing-firm employees placed with an unrelated client. Permitted combinations are also possible.

Use the definitions in the regulation consistently for the plan year. A job title, “management,” or a hand-picked list is not automatically a permitted ICHRA class. Special rules allow some new-hire transitions from a traditional group plan; have that transition documented before using it.

What does “same terms within a class” mean?

Employees in a class must receive the same benefit terms. A straightforward example is $400 per month for every eligible full-time employee, with the same covered expenses and reimbursement rules.

Allowances may vary by age or number of covered dependents if the regulatory conditions are satisfied. Age-based amounts generally cannot exceed a 3:1 oldest-to-youngest ratio within the class, with the same amount for participants of the same age and consistent family-size adjustments. Document the formula in advance.

Different permitted classes can receive different allowances. You may offer a traditional group plan to one class and an ICHRA to another, but cannot give employees in the same class a choice between those two benefits. Also evaluate the separate Section 105(h) tax nondiscrimination rules; class compliance alone is not a blanket exemption.

When do the 10-employee / 10% / 20-employee minimums apply?

Only certain splits between a traditional group plan and an ICHRA trigger these minimums. They do not impose a ten-employee minimum on an employer offering only an ICHRA.

  • Salaried/non-salaried ICHRA classes generally trigger the rule when another class receives a traditional group plan.
  • Full-time/part-time classes trigger it when one receives the traditional group plan and the other receives the ICHRA.
  • Rating-area classes generally trigger it, except a class defined by an entire state or multiple entire states.
  • Combinations involving an applicable class can trigger it; waiting-period combinations and certain new-hire transitions have special exceptions.
Expected employer size at plan-year startMinimum ICHRA class, when applicable
Fewer than 100 employees10 employees

Count employees offered the ICHRA in the class, not just those who enroll. For example, an 80-person employer offering group coverage to salaried staff cannot use a separate eight-person hourly ICHRA class where the minimum is ten.

Check these cases before finalizing the offer: sole proprietors, partners, and more-than-2% S-corporation shareholders generally are not eligible for the ordinary tax-free employee treatment described here. Family attribution rules can matter. Do not exclude Medicare-eligible workers just because this guide focuses on ACA coverage; an ICHRA can integrate with Medicare under separate rules, and Medicare secondary payer requirements need review.

If you are an applicable large employer (ALE)

Generally, an employer averaging at least 50 full-time employees, including full-time equivalents, in the preceding year is an ALE; related employers are aggregated. Analyze the required offer to at least 95% of full-time employees and their dependents, affordability, and potential employer shared-responsibility payments. A flat allowance is not automatically affordable.

Use the applicable year’s IRS rules and relevant lowest-cost silver plan data. Employee premium-tax-credit affordability generally uses household income; employer safe harbors are a separate analysis. Record the method, data, ages, locations, and monthly offer amounts used.

03 / The document packet

Adopt the plan. Explain the benefit.

There is no single IRS “ICHRA application” that creates the whole arrangement. Your employer-specific written terms and employee disclosures serve different purposes.

1. Written ICHRA plan document · All sponsors

What to obtain: a document expressly establishing an individual coverage HRA, tailored to your employer. A QSEHRA form, generic group-plan HRA, or this webpage is not a substitute.

What it must address: sponsor and administrator; plan year and effective date; eligible classes and dependents; funding and allowance formula; reimbursable expenses; coverage substantiation; claim and appeal procedures; opt-out rights; carryover/forfeiture; leave, termination and continuation provisions; amendment and termination authority.

How to adopt it: have the authorized employer representative execute the document and any required adoption agreement or company resolution before the benefit operates. Retain the signed version and amendments. Use a benefits document professional to tailor the legal provisions; signing an unreviewed internet template is not a compliance determination.

2. Summary Plan Description (SPD) · ERISA plans

ERISA is the Employee Retirement Income Security Act, the federal law governing most private-employer benefit plans, including many small-business and nonprofit plans. Government plans and most church plans are generally exempt. Small size alone does not exempt a private employer’s ICHRA.

How to prepare the SPD: translate the adopted plan’s terms into a plain-language booklet employees can understand. Use the DOL’s required content rules. Include the plan and employer names, employer EIN and plan number, administrator and agent for legal service, plan year, funding, eligibility, benefits, exclusions, loss-of-benefit rules, claim/appeal process, continuation rights and the prescribed statement of ERISA rights.

An example benefit explanation is: “The plan reimburses up to $400 of eligible individual insurance premiums per month after you submit the required coverage and expense records.” That sentence is only one part of an SPD. A combined plan document/SPD can work if it meets both sets of requirements.

Delivery: generally within 90 days after becoming a participant, or within 120 days after a new plan becomes subject to ERISA. Follow applicable electronic-delivery rules or use paper; keep delivery evidence. Calendar updated SPDs and summaries of material modifications.

3. ICHRA notice + participation / opt-out record · All sponsors

Customize the DOL model notice with the allowance, family eligibility, effective dates, plan year and contact information. Keep the required explanations about individual coverage, proof, enrollment, premium tax credits and opting out. Include the individual-policy ERISA safe-harbor notice where applicable.

Give eligible employees the notice generally at least 90 calendar days before each plan year. Employees who become eligible later must generally receive it no later than their eligibility date. An employer established less than 120 days before its first ICHRA year has a special start-date exception. Merely creating a new ICHRA at an established employer does not create that exception.

Give participants an opportunity to opt out and waive future reimbursements at least annually. On termination, the plan must provide for forfeiture or a permanent opt-out/waiver of remaining amounts, as required by the rule. Keep participation and waiver records.

4. Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) · ICHRA benefit summary

Prepare the ICHRA’s SBC using the federal template and instructions, adapted to the reimbursement benefit, with the Uniform Glossary access information. The insurer’s policy SBC describes the insurance; it does not replace the employer’s ICHRA disclosure.

Provide the SBC with application/enrollment materials and at renewal, and generally within seven business days of a request. Follow the special-enrollment rules. Material changes affecting the SBC outside renewal generally require 60 days’ advance notice.

DOL health-plan templates and guidance →

5. Claim forms, attestations, ledger & appeals · All sponsors

Prepare annual and ongoing coverage attestation forms, a premium reimbursement request, a no-duplicate-reimbursement statement, an approval/denial record and a ledger. The DOL model attestations can be customized; remove the instructions page from the employee form.

Write where to submit claims, required evidence, filing deadlines, who reviews, how payment is made and how to appeal. Typical ERISA post-service claims must be decided within 30 days, subject to a permitted extension. Denials must explain the reason, plan provisions, missing information if applicable, and appeal rights. Allow at least 180 days to appeal; a single-level appeal generally must be decided within 60 days. Have the document preparer address other applicable claims/appeals and external-review requirements.

6. Section 125 plan + salary-reduction elections · If offering pretax payroll

Adopt or amend a separate written cafeteria plan that specifically permits unreimbursed off-exchange individual premiums alongside the ICHRA. Include eligibility, benefits, plan year, election procedures and permitted changes. Obtain employees’ timely elections before affected compensation becomes available. See the payroll setup steps below.

7. Conditional notices, privacy policies & reporting · Check your employer
  • COBRA: generally applies at 20 or more employees under its counting rules. An ICHRA is a group health plan for this purpose. Set up initial notices, qualifying-event tracking and election notices; determine continuation pricing and rights under the actual plan. The individual policy’s continuation is separate.
  • HIPAA privacy/security: a group health plan with fewer than 50 participants administered solely by its employer is excluded from the HIPAA health-plan definition. Otherwise evaluate privacy notices, policies, designated officials, security safeguards, business associate agreements and plan-document requirements. Protect employee information even where that exception applies.
  • Marketplace coverage-options notice: FLSA-covered employers generally provide this to new employees within 14 days of hire, whether or not they qualify for the ICHRA.
  • ERISA changes and reports: calendar updated SPDs, summaries of material modifications, any Form 5500 and related participant reports. A material reduction in health benefits generally has a shorter disclosure deadline.
  • Other design/state duties: identify applicable leave and continuation rules, state payroll deduction rules, state coverage reporting, dependent-coverage and other group-health disclosures. Adding medical-expense reimbursement or other benefits can change the review.

Ask the document preparer to produce a written applicability checklist for your actual plan and employer. Do not assume every traditional group-insurance notice applies to the HRA in the same way.

04 / Notice & enrollment

Align the dates before coverage starts.

  1. Employer: deliver the customized notice and enrollment packet on time. Explain the allowance, employee responsibility, opt-out process and any Section 125 election deadline.
  2. Employee and agent: confirm open enrollment or a valid special enrollment period. Newly gaining ICHRA access can create an SEP; coordinate carrier deadlines and effective-date rules. Do not cancel existing coverage until the replacement is confirmed.
  3. Employee: choose qualifying individual ACA coverage, confirm it is actually off-exchange, pay the required initial premium and complete the ICHRA participation forms.
  4. Employer: obtain annual coverage substantiation for every person covered by the HRA, using insurer records or a signed attestation of coverage that is or will be in force. Complete this by the applicable HRA coverage start under the rule.
  5. Both: align the insurance start date, ICHRA eligibility date and salary-reduction election. Reimburse only expenses within eligible periods and the written plan’s rules.

Subsidy conversation first. Off-exchange coverage does not qualify for Marketplace premium tax credits. An affordable ICHRA offer can block credits for an offered individual even if declined. If the offer is unaffordable, the individual generally must opt out of the ICHRA to receive any otherwise-available credit through the Marketplace. Household members’ eligibility depends on their own offers and circumstances.

To preserve the individual-policy ERISA safe harbor, participation in the policy must be voluntary, employees must select their individual coverage, and the employer must not select or endorse a particular issuer/policy or receive consideration connected to the selection or renewal. Give the required notice that the individual policy is not subject to ERISA’s protections. The ICHRA itself generally remains an ERISA plan for a private employer.

05 / Monthly administration

Verify two things. Then reimburse.

Coverage verification answers “Was this person insured for this month?” Expense substantiation answers “What eligible premium are we paying?” You need both.

Example claim packetWhat it establishes
Insurer invoice or premium statementEmployee/policy identification, covered period, covered people as relevant, and the $700 premium amount.
Insurer receipt or payment confirmation, if employee paid firstPayment connected to that policy and coverage period. A bank debit alone may not identify the policy, month or eligible expense.
Ongoing coverage attestation or other permitted proofThe person for whom reimbursement is requested was covered in the month the expense was incurred. An annual attestation alone does not satisfy each later claim.
Signed reimbursement requestRequested amount and confirmation that the expense has not been reimbursed elsewhere and will not be claimed again.
Employer approval and ledger entryEligible amount, ICHRA portion, any employee-funded portion, reviewer, payment date, and remaining allowance.

Example: The employee provides a $700 monthly invoice, payment confirmation and the month’s coverage attestation. With a $400 available allowance, the employer approves $400 and records it as a tax-free ICHRA reimbursement. The other $300 is handled separately under the Section 125 plan, if offered, or paid after tax.

  • Follow the plan’s reasonable substantiation procedures before every reimbursement. Direct premium payment can be a permitted way to substantiate ongoing coverage; retain the supporting invoice and payment record.
  • You may rely on an attestation unless you have actual knowledge that it is incorrect. Stop reimbursements for ineligible coverage periods and resolve cancellations or refunds.
  • Keep health and claim records in restricted files, separate from routine personnel files. Limit access to the people administering the plan.
  • Unused allowances follow the written carryover/forfeiture rules. They cannot be paid out as unrestricted cash.

A pay-stub line is a payment method, not the plan. Payroll can show an approved tax-free ICHRA reimbursement after the required review. Simply labeling an automatic cash payment “ICHRA” does not make it tax-free or compliant.

06 / Optional Section 125

Make the employee’s balance pretax.

Yes: an eligible employee with an ICHRA and an off-exchange ACA policy can pay the remaining premium through pretax payroll when the employer offers a compliant Section 125 cafeteria plan.

A cafeteria plan allows employees to choose qualified benefits instead of taxable wages. The employee cannot elect this tax treatment independently. The employer’s ICHRA money and the employee’s salary reductions must remain separately tracked.

  1. Adopt the written Section 125 plan. Expressly permit the remaining individual off-exchange premium alongside the ICHRA. A generic document limited to employer group-policy premiums may need amendment.
  2. Confirm eligibility and obtain the election. Record policy, premium, employer reimbursement, employee balance, payroll frequency, per-pay deduction, start date and authorization before the affected pay becomes available. Do not retroactively convert wages already available into pretax contributions.
  3. Configure payroll and payment. Create separate codes for employer ICHRA reimbursement and employee pretax premium deductions. Verify applicable federal/state wage treatment and state deduction authorization. Arrange documented payment of the full premium to the insurer.
  4. Choose one reconciled payment method. The employer can coordinate insurer payment, or use a properly documented reimbursement process when the employee pays first. If reimbursing both portions, identify which is employer-funded and which comes from the employee’s elected salary reduction. Do not also pay the carrier for the same premium.
  5. Administer the elections and tests. Apply permitted midyear election-change rules and the written plan’s terms; test applicable Section 125 nondiscrimination and key-employee limits. Correct errors with qualified payroll/benefits advice.

Three firm boundaries: employee deductions do not fund the ICHRA; a full-price Marketplace policy is still on-exchange and does not qualify for this individual-premium pretax option; and a health FSA is not a substitute for the premium-payment benefit.

Without a Section 125 plan, the employee generally pays the remaining premium after tax. Sole proprietors, partners and more-than-2% S-corporation shareholders generally cannot participate in the cafeteria plan as employees. Evaluate family attribution and other eligibility issues before applying deductions.

07 / Tax & reporting calendar

Plan for the filings from day one.

These are separate from the employer’s ordinary income-tax return. Assign a responsible person and confirm the instructions for the actual reporting year. Collect covered individuals’ identifying information securely throughout the year.

Electronic filing is generally required when the filer has at least 10 information returns in aggregate across covered return types, not just 1095 forms, unless an exception or waiver applies. Set up an appropriate ACA filing method early.

What does the employee file?

An employee generally does not file a special federal form solely because of tax-free ICHRA reimbursements or valid Section 125 premium deductions. Keep the W-2, coverage statements and supporting records. Forms 1095-B/C generally are retained, not attached to the federal return.

A policy purchased entirely off-exchange does not generate a Marketplace Form 1095-A or premium tax credit for that policy. If the household had Marketplace coverage during another part of the year, review Form 1095-A and any required Form 8962 reconciliation. Do not deduct or reimburse again premiums already paid tax-free. HSA participation, if applicable, creates separate reporting such as Form 8889.

No separate annual IRS return solely for the cafeteria plan. Its benefits and payroll still have to satisfy the tax rules. Keep plan documents, elections, nondiscrimination results, claim approvals, notices and tax filings under a documented retention schedule. ERISA report-support records generally must be kept at least six years; other records can require longer retention.

08 / Keep the benefit running

Build a repeatable routine.

Every reimbursement cycle

Review, pay, reconcile

Check eligibility, coverage and premium evidence. Approve the eligible amount, process payment, reconcile the insurer and payroll records, update balances and follow up on missing information.

Every plan year

Review, notify, renew

Recheck classes, allowances, affordability and nondiscrimination. Adopt changes, issue timely notices, obtain opt-outs/elections and renewed attestations, and complete reporting.

When something changes

Track hires, terminations, leave, dependent changes, cancellations, premium refunds and address changes. Review how each affects eligibility, the available allowance, coverage verification, payroll elections, continuation rights and tax reporting. A termination does not automatically cancel the employee’s individual insurance.

Communicate with the employee about who pays the full premium after employer support ends. Handle any approved run-out claims or continuation under the written plan, and reconcile outstanding salary reductions promptly.

Quick answers

Questions employers ask.

Can we just add $400 to everyone’s pay?

A genuine unconditional wage increase is generally taxable compensation. It is not a tax-free ICHRA. If the payment is conditioned on buying insurance or reimburses premiums, additional health-plan rules apply; calling it taxable does not automatically resolve those rules. Use the documented ICHRA process for the benefit described here.

IRS Notice 2015-17 →

Is “CHOICE” a different type of plan?

CMS uses CHOICE Arrangement as the name for the individual coverage HRA. This guide uses ICHRA so employers can match the terminology in the regulations, notices and tax guidance. The name does not eliminate the underlying compliance duties.

Does an agent or HealthSherpa handle the administration?

Not merely by helping someone enroll. An agent or enrollment platform can assist with policy selection and enrollment. The employer still needs someone to administer the ICHRA and satisfy its legal and reporting duties, whether internally or under a separate service agreement. HealthSherpa’s ICHRA article describes enrollment support, not an employer plan-administration service.

HealthSherpa’s ICHRA overview →

Can employees keep an HSA?

A premium-only ICHRA can preserve HSA eligibility for an otherwise eligible person with qualifying HSA-compatible coverage and no disqualifying coverage. An HRA that reimburses general medical expenses before the required deductible can change that. Verify the policy and HRA terms, Medicare status, and current tax rules before making HSA contributions.

IRS final rule: HSA coordination →

Can an ICHRA reimburse a Marketplace plan?

Yes, a qualifying on-exchange plan can integrate with an ICHRA, subject to the rules and tax-credit restrictions. This resource focuses on off-exchange plans because the individual premium balance can be paid through the employer’s Section 125 plan. A Marketplace plan without subsidies is still on-exchange.

Does the ICHRA have every filing a traditional group plan has?

No. Apply the rules to the benefit actually offered. For example, federal agencies currently do not enforce the gag-clause attestation requirement against plans consisting solely of HRAs. That relief does not remove 1095 reporting, PCORI, or other duties that apply. If the employer also offers other benefits, review their separate obligations.
